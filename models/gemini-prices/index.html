Reading the cells

  1. Dates. For example Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon are $2.00 input and $10.00 output through 30 November 2026, and $4.00 and $20.00 from 1 December 2026. The cost comparison on the home page applies the date of your device.
  2. Prompt size. Gemini 2.5 Pro has a higher price for prompts above 200k tokens. The comparison uses the lower price.
  3. Kinds of input. Some cells list a separate price for audio or video. The comparison uses the text price.
  4. Other tabs. The page also has Batch and Flex prices and free tier terms: this site does not include them.
  5. Not listed here. Image, audio, speech, live and embedding models are on the page too.

The page states the dates but not the reasons.
