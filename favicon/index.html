How to use it

  1. Type a letter or two, or choose a picture. Pick the colours and the shape.
  2. Look at the previews, especially the 16 pixel one.
  3. Press "Download the pack", and put the files in the folder of your site that you named.
  4. Paste the tags into the <head> of your pages.

What you get

A favicon.ico file with three sizes, PNG files for tabs, the iPhone home screen and Android, an optional maskable icon, a web app manifest and a text file with the tags.

Good to know

Pictures are drawn in your browser, so nothing is uploaded. If your picture is a detailed logo, test it at 16 pixels: a letter or a bold mark often works better.

Can I use my logo as it is?

Yes, if it is mostly square. The tool fits it inside a square without stretching it.

Is a transparent background possible?

Not fully. The icons are drawn on the background colour you choose. Only the corners outside a rounded or round shape are transparent. The maskable icon and the iPhone icon are always filled with the colour.
