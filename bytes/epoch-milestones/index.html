How the table was made

Each date was calculated with Python's datetime module by adding the number of seconds to 1 January 1970 00:00:00 UTC. The table is a quick way to get a feel for how large timestamps are: today's values are above 1.7 billion, and the signed 32-bit limit of 2,147,483,647 falls on 19 January 2038. The negative entry is the smallest value of a signed 32-bit integer, which lands in December 1901.

To convert any other number, use the epoch converter. To understand what happens at the 2038 limit, read the Year 2038 page.
