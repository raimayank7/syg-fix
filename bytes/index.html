How to avoid the usual mistakes

  1. Seconds or milliseconds? A 10-digit timestamp is seconds, a 13-digit one is milliseconds.
  2. Which time zone? A time without a zone is ambiguous. Write the offset or use UTC.
  3. Bits or bytes? Speeds are in bits, files in bytes. Divide Mbit/s by 8.
  4. 1,000 or 1,024? KB and KiB are different units.

Frequently asked questions

Are the converters accurate?

We checked them against Python on 1,100 random cases and found no differences.

Does the site know my clock?

The "now" values come from your own device. The site does not send them anywhere.

Which time zone database is used?

The one built into your browser, which follows the IANA database.
