CSS gradient presets

20 gradients you can copy. Each is a complete background declaration.

  • Sunset

    Warm orange and peach for hero sections and buttons.

  • Ocean

    background: linear-gradient(135deg, #2193b0 0%, #6dd5ed 100%);

  • Night sky

    background: linear-gradient(160deg, #0f2027 0%, #203a43 50%, #2c5364 100%);

  • Mint

    Fresh green to light blue.

    background: linear-gradient(120deg, #84fab0 0%, #8fd3f4 100%);

  • Peach

    Soft background for cards and forms.

    background: linear-gradient(135deg, #ffecd2 0%, #fcb69f 100%);

  • Royal

    Blue to violet, popular for app landing pages.

    background: linear-gradient(135deg, #4776e6 0%, #8e54e9 100%);

    Amber to yellow for highlights.

    Dark fade at the bottom of a photo so white text is readable.

  • Half and half

    Hard stop: two flat colours side by side.

    background: linear-gradient(90deg, #f4b400 50%, #1b2a49 50%);

How to use these

Press Copy on a card and paste the line into the rule of your element. Every gradient here was checked in current Chrome and WebKit on 13 November 2026. To change the colours or the angle, open the gradient generator and type your own, or read the guide to gradients to understand each part.

Check the contrast of your text against the whole gradient, not only against the colour at one end. Light gradients such as Mint and Peach need dark text, and dark ones such as Night sky and Plum need light text. The Photo fade is meant to sit on top of a picture, as a second background layer, to make white text readable.
