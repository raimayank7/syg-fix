Laptop keyboard or touchpad not working

Until it is fixed you can work with a USB keyboard or mouse, or with the on-screen keyboard: Settings › Accessibility › Keyboard.

What you seeKeys do nothing, type numbers for letters, or the pointer will not move

Touchpad not working

  1. The touchpad switch. Many laptops turn the touchpad off with a function key: look along the top row for a picture of a touchpad, often Fn + F6, F7, F9 or F10, or a double-tap on a dot in the touchpad's corner. Press it once.
  2. Settings. Open Settings with Windows key + I, use Tab and the arrow keys, and go to Bluetooth & devices › Touchpad. Turn Touchpad On. Tick "Leave touchpad on when a mouse is connected" if the pad stops whenever a mouse is plugged in.
  3. Restart the laptop.
  4. Clean it. A damp, greasy or dusty pad misreads fingers. Wipe with a slightly damp cloth, dry, and try with dry hands.
  5. Driver. Right-click Start (or press Windows key + X) › Device Manager › Mice and other pointing devices or Human Interface Devices. Right-click the touchpad › Uninstall device › restart. Or install the touchpad driver for your model from the maker's support page.
  6. After an update: Properties › Driver › Roll Back Driver.

Whole keyboard not working

  1. Restart. If keys work in the BIOS set-up screen or on the sign-in screen but not later, the cause is software.
  2. Drain the power: shut down, unplug, hold the power button 30 seconds, start.
  3. Driver: Device Manager › Keyboards › right-click the keyboard › Uninstall device › restart.
  4. Filter Keys. Settings › Accessibility › Keyboard › turn Filter keys and Sticky keys Off. Filter Keys makes Windows ignore brief key presses; it switches on if the right Shift key is held for eight seconds.
  5. Test in Safe Mode. If the keyboard works in Safe Mode, a program or driver you installed is interfering.
  6. External keyboard test. If a USB keyboard works and the built-in one does not, even in the BIOS, the laptop keyboard or its ribbon cable has failed: a service job.

Some keys not working

  • Dirt under keys: switch off, turn the laptop upside down and tap gently; blow between the keys with a hand blower. Do not prise off keys on thin laptops, as the clips break.
  • A liquid spill: switch off at once, unplug, turn upside down to drain and leave it off for a day or more. Sugary drinks need a service centre.
  • A row or block of dead keys usually means a damaged keyboard, not software.

Wireless keyboards and mice

Replace the batteries, re-plug the receiver into another port close to the device, and pair again. See Bluetooth not working.

How do I sign in if the keyboard is dead?

On the sign-in screen, click the Accessibility icon at the bottom right and turn on the On-Screen Keyboard, then click your password or PIN.

Why does the touchpad stop while I type?

That is palm rejection. Its strength is under Settings › Bluetooth & devices › Touchpad › Taps › Touchpad sensitivity.
