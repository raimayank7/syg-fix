Cron expression for every hour during business hours

Every hour, on the hour, between 09:00 and 17:00, on weekdays (Monday to Friday).

Five fields for Linux, GitHub Actions and Kubernetes (minute hour day month weekday). Six or seven for Quartz. Nothing you type leaves your device.

What it means and when to use it

A range such as 9-17 in the hour field lists every hour from 9 to 17 inclusive. With minute 0 that is nine runs a day: 9:00, 10:00 and so on up to 17:00. Add 1-5 for the working days. This is the standard way to say "every hour during the working day" for status emails, ticket checks, or a polling job that is pointless at night.

Watch out for this

If you change minute 0 to */15, the range 9-17 still includes the whole of hour 17, so you get runs at 17:15, 17:30 and 17:45 as well. The hour range limits the hour of the day, not the final minute. To end exactly at 17:00 you need two lines (see the page on every 15 minutes). The range also contains no time zone: 9-17 means the server's clock.

The same schedule in other places

Quartz

Quartz has a seconds field in front, uses ? in one day field, and counts Sunday as 1:

0 0 9-17 ? * 2-6

GitHub Actions

Scheduled workflows run in UTC unless you add a time zone. In the workflow file:

on:
  schedule:
    - cron: '0 9-17 * * 1-5'

Kubernetes CronJob

In a CronJob manifest the schedule goes in spec.schedule. Without spec.timeZone, it is read in the time zone of the cluster's controller:

spec:
  schedule: "0 9-17 * * 1-5"
  timeZone: "Etc/UTC"

A crontab line to copy

The command is made up. Replace it with your own and use full paths:

0 9-17 * * 1-5 /home/me/bin/ticket-check.sh >> /home/me/tickets.log 2>&1

Questions

Is the range inclusive?

Yes, the manual says the specified range is inclusive: 8-11 means hours 8, 9, 10 and 11.
