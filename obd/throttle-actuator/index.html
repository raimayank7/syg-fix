P2100 to P2119

Electronic throttle actuator

Fault codes P2100 to P2119 explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: HighThis can damage the engine or gearbox, or leave you stranded. Stop if the car feels unsafe, and have it checked now.

What it means

On an electronic throttle a small motor opens and closes the throttle valve. These codes mean the motor is not moving the valve the way the computer commands, or the computer has put the engine into a limited mode because of a throttle fault.

Bank 1 is the side of the engine that has cylinder 1. Sensor 1 is before the catalytic converter and sensor 2 is after it. More terms explained.

What you may notice

  • Engine power limited, sometimes only idle speed
  • High or low idle
  • Stalling when coming to a stop

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • Carbon build-up making the throttle valve stick
  • A failed throttle body motor
  • Wiring or connector faults
  • Low battery voltage

What to check first

  • Check the battery is healthy: weak batteries cause odd throttle faults
  • A workshop can clean the throttle body and relearn its positions
  • Do not push the throttle plate by hand on an electronic throttle: it can damage the gears

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.
