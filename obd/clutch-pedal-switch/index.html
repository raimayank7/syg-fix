P0805 to P0833

Clutch pedal position sensor (manual gearbox)

Fault codes P0805 to P0833 explained in plain words: what the car is telling you, how serious it is and what to check first.

How serious: LowUsually safe to keep driving. Repair it when convenient, and do not ignore it for months.

What it means

Manual cars have a switch or sensor on the clutch pedal. It lets the engine start only with the clutch pressed, cancels cruise control, and helps the computer smooth gear changes and run stop-start.

Bank 1 is the side of the engine that has cylinder 1. Sensor 1 is before the catalytic converter and sensor 2 is after it. More terms explained.

What you may notice

  • Engine will not crank unless the pedal is pushed very hard, or at all
  • Cruise control not working
  • Stop-start not working
  • Jerky revs when changing gear

Common causes

Most likely first:

  • A worn or misadjusted pedal switch
  • A broken clip or stopper on the pedal
  • Wiring fault

What to check first

  • Press the clutch pedal fully to the floor when starting
  • Look under the dash for a switch hanging loose from the pedal bracket

Have a mechanic confirm. A fault code tells you which system reported a problem, not which part has failed. The same code can have several causes, and they differ between car models. Use this page to understand the code, then have a qualified mechanic test the car before any part is replaced.

After the repair

Once the cause is fixed, the code can be cleared with a scan tool, or the warning light goes out by itself after several trips without the fault. Clearing a code without fixing the cause only hides it for a while. More about clearing codes.
