Series and parallel resistor calculator

Type two or more resistor values separated by spaces or commas.

The rules

  • Series: R = R1 + R2 + R3 ... The same current flows through all of them.
  • Parallel: 1 / R = 1 / R1 + 1 / R2 + 1 / R3 ... The same voltage is across all of them. The total is always smaller than the smallest resistor.

Making a value you do not have

Two equal resistors in parallel give half the value, and in series twice the value. For example two 100 Ω in parallel make 50 Ω. To make a value that is not in the E-series, combine two resistors in series or parallel and check the result here.

How it was tested

We compared the results with direct calculation for sets of values. Examples: 100 and 100 in parallel give 50 Ω, and 1 k, 2 k and 3 k in parallel give 545.5 Ω.

Is there a formula for two in parallel?

Yes: R1 × R2 / (R1 + R2).

Does the total power add up?

The total power is the sum of what each resistor dissipates.
