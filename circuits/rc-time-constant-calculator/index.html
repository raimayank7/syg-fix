RC time constant calculator

Enter the resistor and the capacitor. You get the time constant and the cut-off frequency of the RC filter.

The formulas

  • Time constant: τ = R × C, in seconds when R is in ohms and C in farads.
  • Cut-off frequency of a simple RC filter: f = 1 / (2π R C).
  • Rise time from 10% to 90%: ln(9) × τ, which is about 2.197 τ.
  • Time to reach 99%: ln(100) × τ, about 4.6 τ.

After one time constant a charging capacitor is at about 63% of the final voltage.

Writing capacitor values

Use u for micro, n for nano and p for pico: 100n is 100 nanofarad (0.1 µF), and 2u2 is 2.2 µF.

How it was tested

300 random R and C pairs, from 10 ohm to 1 megaohm and 10 picofarad to 1 millifarad, were compared with direct calculation.

Is this the cut-off of a high-pass or low-pass?

The same value applies to a simple RC low-pass and high-pass filter: it is the frequency where the output is about 70.7% of the input.

Does the tool include the tolerance of parts?

No. Real resistors and especially capacitors can be off by 5 to 20 percent or more: check their data sheets.
