Free or paid AI: how to decide without wasting money

Pay when a limit costs you more than the plan would. Until then the free plan is enough for most office use.

What paid plans usually add

Plans differ by company and change often. In general, paid plans give some of: more messages before a limit, access to more powerful models, longer files and conversations, extra tools (code, data analysis, research, image or video features), and priority when many people are using the service. Business and team plans add account management and, usually, stricter data terms.

What one maker's page said

On 13 November 2026 Anthropic's plans page listed a Free plan, Pro, Max and Ultra for individuals, and Team and Enterprise plans for organisations. Its comparison table showed that the Free plan had limited usage, and that the paid individual plans offered 1 times, 5 times and more usage relative to Pro, plus extra features such as research, a code tool and connections to Microsoft 365 for the paid plans. The page also said that prices are shown without tax and that prices and plans can change at the company's discretion. We do not copy prices here, because they change; open the official page for the current figures and see the same kind of page for any other tool.

The decision test

  1. Use the free plan for two weeks on your real work.
  2. Write down every time you hit a limit, wait, or need a feature that is missing.
  3. Estimate the time lost. If it costs you more than the plan costs, pay for one month and see.
  4. After the month, look at whether you used the extra features. If not, cancel.

Warning signs of a bad deal

  • A "lifetime" or very cheap plan from a site that is not the company itself.
  • An app that looks like the real one but is sold by someone else. Download only from the company's own link or the official app store page.
  • Annual plans that you cannot cancel. Start monthly.
  • Pressure ("only today"). The tools do not run out.

Taxes, currency and payment

Prices are often shown in dollars and without tax. Check the final amount, the currency and the renewal date before you pay, and set a reminder to cancel if you only need it for a project.

Workplace plans

If your employer pays, it is usually under a business or team plan with its own data terms. Use that account for work and keep your personal account for personal use; do not mix them.

Students and teachers

Some companies offer education pricing. Check the company's own education page, not a third-party deal site.

Is paid always better?

For heavy daily use, often. For occasional drafting, the free plan is usually enough.

Can I switch later?

Yes. Your old chats may or may not move with you, so export what you need.
