AI for shopkeepers and small businesses: messages, listings and offers

A shopkeeper has no time to write. AI can write a message in ten seconds. You still decide the price, the promise and the tone.

What helps a small business

  • WhatsApp messages for offers, reminders, thanks and "your order is ready".
  • Product descriptions for an online listing, from your facts.
  • Replies to customer questions, polite and short.
  • Festival and season ideas, with simple offers you decide.
  • Poster text and simple pictures (see the image tools page).
  • A simple price list layout or a menu card text.
  • Replies to reviews, including a polite answer to a complaint.

Prompts you can adapt

Offer message. "Write three WhatsApp messages (each under 40 words) for a mobile accessories shop in Ludhiana: 10% off on earphones until Sunday. Friendly tone, one emoji at most, end with the shop timing 10 am to 8 pm. No words in capital letters."

Product description. "Write a 60-word description for an online listing of a steel water bottle, 1 litre, keeps cold for 12 hours [only if true], 2-year seller warranty [only if true], available in blue and black. No exaggeration, no claims that I did not give."

Reply to a complaint. "A customer wrote that the shirt shrank after one wash. Write a polite reply of under 70 words that apologises once, asks for the bill number and offers to look at the shirt in the shop. Do not promise a refund."

Hindi message. "Write a short WhatsApp message in simple Hindi (Devanagari) thanking a customer for shopping and inviting them back. Keep English words like 'bill' and 'offer'. Add the English meaning below."

Rules for honest selling

  • Only claim what is true. Do not let a tool add "best in India", "100% pure" or a warranty that you do not give. Customers and the law hold you to the words.
  • Check prices and dates in every message before sending.
  • Do not paste customers' phone numbers and addresses into a public tool. For a mailing, write the text with AI and send it through your own contact list.
  • Do not use fake reviews. Do not ask AI to write "customer testimonials".
  • Label AI pictures that are not real photos of your products, and never show an AI picture as the real item you sell.

Save time with templates

Write one good message per situation (offer, reminder, thanks, complaint). Save it in your phone's notes or WhatsApp Business quick replies and change only the details. AI helps you write the templates once; after that you do not need it for each message.

Know the limits

AI does not know your stock, your prices or your customers. It cannot see your shop. It can be wrong about local rules (GST, labelling). Ask your accountant or the official site for rules.

Can I put AI answers directly on my WhatsApp Business auto-reply?

Check the platform's rules and test every answer. A wrong promise to a customer is your promise.
