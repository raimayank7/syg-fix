How to use the table

  • For personal use, the safest habit is the same for all: keep private identifiers out, use the temporary or incognito mode for sensitive questions, and turn off the training option if you do not want it.
  • For work and clients, use a business or organisation plan that your employer has approved. Read its terms.
  • Check the date. These pages change. Open the originals before you decide.

What is common to all of them

None of the companies' pages promises that a chat is secret. Chats are stored on servers, may be reviewed under some conditions, and can be exposed by account theft. The right standard is not "is it private?" but "would I be able to live with this being read?".

Which is the most private?

The pages do not allow a ranking. They describe different controls. Pick the one whose controls you understand and have set.

Do these rules apply in India?

The pages we read are global or US-oriented. Indian law also applies to how businesses handle personal data; see the DPDP Act page.
