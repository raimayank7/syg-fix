AI at Work

A prompt library for work: 30 prompts by job, ready to copy

Copy a prompt, replace the words in square brackets with your own facts, and read the answer carefully. Never put private data in the brackets.

These prompts are our own wording. We have not run them against every tool, and results differ between tools and over time. Always read and check the answer.

Office and email

ForPromptCheck
Polite reminder"Write a polite reminder email of under 70 words asking [role] to send [item] by [date]. Friendly but clear."Date and item
Decline a request"Write a short, respectful email declining [request] because [reason]. Offer [alternative]. Under 80 words."Reason is true
Meeting minutes"Turn these notes into minutes with headings: decisions, action items (owner, date), open questions. Add nothing that is not in the notes: [notes without private details]"Compare with notes
Shorten"Shorten this to half its length and keep every number and date: [text]"Numbers unchanged
Simplify"Rewrite this for a reader who is not an expert, in short sentences: [text]"Meaning unchanged
Subject lines"Give me 8 subject lines under 8 words for an email about [topic]."Honest, not clickbait

Make your own

Keep what works. Write down the prompt, the tool and the date, and what you changed afterwards.

Do I need special words like "act as"?

Not usually. Say what you want, for whom and in what form.

Why does the same prompt give different answers?

Tools produce text with some randomness, and models change over time.
